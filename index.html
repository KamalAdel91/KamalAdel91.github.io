Who I Am

About Me

I started my career in accounting and tax. That background is my edge: I understand how money actually moves through a business, how auditors think, and why a report that doesn't reconcile is worse than no report at all.

Over time I moved into business intelligence, ERP and automation, building the systems I always wished finance teams had. Today I design and deliver complete solutions: from implementing the ERP, to migrating years of historical data, to building the dashboards that sit on top.

I work with multi-company groups in Egypt and Saudi Arabia, handling multiple entities, currencies and tax regimes, including ZATCA e-invoicing compliance.

Alongside client work, I build my own suite of business applications and teach accounting, Excel, Power BI, ERP and AI through free educational content.
